About me

A little about
who I am

I'm a Palm Beach based creative with a deep love for beauty, the written word, and the slow art of living beautifully. The girl who spends too long putting together the perfect look, loses herself in a good book, and finds inspiration in a museum at noon on a Tuesday.

A little quiet, a little social — I come alive around the right people, in the right rooms, at the right golden hour. Travel fills my soul, music sets the mood, and art reminds me why details matter.

This space was born from a love of all things beautiful — the way a morning routine becomes a ritual, the way a single fragrance can take you somewhere entirely else.
